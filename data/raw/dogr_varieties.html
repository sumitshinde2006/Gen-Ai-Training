Bhima Light Red

Medium maturing variety with light red globe bulbs, thin neck, and excellent storage qualities.

Recommended States: Karnataka, Tamil Nadu
Season:
Rabi
Maturity: 115 days after transplanting
Yield: 385 q/ha

Bhima Dark Red

Bhima Dark Red

Plant: Semi-erect height 55-60cm, 9-11 medium green leaves, leaf diameter 1.4-1.6cm , pseudostem lenth 6-7 cm, pseudostem dialmete 1.2-1.4 cm and no foliage cranking.
Bulb: Dark red, flat-globe, thin neck thicknessm, polar diameter 4.0-4.2 cm, equatorial diameter 4.5-4.0 cm, medium rings purplish flesh, medium adherence of skin, exerted root disc, symmetrical bulb, single axis and non- splitting types
Recommended Season and Region: Chhattisgarh, Delhi, Gujarat, Haryana, Jammu, Karnataka, Madhya Prades, Maharashtra, Orissa, Punjab, Rajasthan and Tamil Nadu.
Season:
Kharif
Maturity: 100-110 days after transplanting
Bolting: Less than 5%
Average Yield: 22-24t/ha
Highest Yield: 32t/ha
Storability: Kharif harvest can be stored up to 2 months
Total Soluble Solides: 10-11° Brix
Reaction to Major Pests: Field tolerant to thrips and foliar diseases
Uniqueness: Bulb attains immediate dark red color after harvest, early and uniform maturity and comparatively less doubles and bolters

Bhima Raj

Bhima Raj

Plant: Erect, height 45-50 cm, 8-12 dark green leaves, leaf diameter 0.8-1.0 cm, pseudostem length 6-8 cm, pseudosem diameter 1.4-1.6 cm and no foliage cranking
Bulb: Dark red, globose, neck thickness medium, polar diameter 4.0-4.5 cm, medium ring raddish flesh, weak adherence of skin, exerted root disc symmetrical bulb single axis and non-splitting types
Recommended Season and Region:
  • For kharif and late kharif : Gujarat, Karnataka and Maharashtra
  • For rabi : Delhi, Gujarat ,Haryana and Rajasthan
Season:
Kharif Late Kharif Rabi
Maturity: 100-105 days transplanting (Kharif ),115-120 days after transplanting (late kharif and rabi)
Bolting: Less than 5% during late kharif and rabi
Average Yield: 24-26t/ha (kharif), 40-50t/ha (late kharif) and 25-30t/ha (rabi)
Highest Yield: 30t/ha (kharif), 45t/ha (late kharif), 40t/ha (rabi)
Storability: Late kharif harvest can be stored up to 4months and rabi harvest for 2-3 months

Reaction to Major Pests: Field tolerant to thrips and foliar diseases
Uniqueness: Attractive dark red bulbs, uniform neck fall, can be grown in all the three seasons

Bhima Prasanskaran

Bhima Prasanskaran

Bhima Prasanskaran (DOGR HT-3) is India’s 1st High-TSS Short day White Onion Rabi Variety Suited for Processing, developed by ICAR- Directorate of Onion and Garlic Research, Rajgurunagar-410505, Pune (Maharashtra) using Mass selection method

Storability: The variety exhibits lower storage losses of 31.11% after four months of storage
Recommended Season and Region:Gujarat, Maharashtra, Karnataka, Telangana, Andhra Pradesh and Tamil Nadu.
Season:
Rabi
Yield: Bulb yield 273.59 q/ha, surpassing the national average yield
Uniqueness:

1st high-TSS short-day white onion variety in India

TSS 17.20 °Brix, Suited for processing and dehydration

Moderately resistant to foliar diseases and tolerant to thrips

Bhima Omkar

Bulb: White, circular shape in longitudinal section & elliptical shape in cross section, compact, diameter 3.0-3.5 cm 18-20 cloves, exerted clove at tip of bulb inserted root disc, recessed base, strong adherence of skin, clove diameter 0.8-1.2 cm, white clove and whitish flesh

Rabi
